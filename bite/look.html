Techniques

Six techniques behind the Bite's aesthetic, what they do and how they feel.
Technique Effect Feeling
Hard flash Flat bright subject, background drops to black, hard shadows behind edges. Candid.
Film grain Visible texture across shadows and skies, strongest in underexposed areas. Nostalgic.
Fisheye lens Extreme wide angle, makes straight lines bend, near objects swell. New Perspective.
Desaturation Colours pulled toward grey and brown, like old prints left in the sun. Forgotten.
Motion blur Slow shutter smears anything moving while still things stay sharp. Chaos.
Underexposure Most of the frame sinks into shadow, only the essential survives. Mysterious.
